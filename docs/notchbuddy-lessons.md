# What CoachRank can learn from NotchBuddy

Reviewed September 20, 2026. This is a strategy assessment, not a claim about the cause of another founder's revenue.

## What the public evidence supports

[TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/notchbuddy) showed $1,006 all-time revenue, no active subscriptions and Dodo API verification, updated September 20 at 12:39 PM. It identified Mohit Vaswani as the founder and listed 7,554 X followers. All-time revenue is not monthly recurring revenue, profit or a traffic-to-purchase conversion rate. We cannot infer customer count by dividing revenue by today's price: prices and purchases may have changed.

The [NotchBuddy website](https://notchbuddy.com/) presents a visible product: 19 Mac utilities, screenshots, a demo and a one-time $5.99 launch offer. It describes specific jobs such as keeping a clipboard history and moving files, and says buyers need no account. It also links public X reactions. These are the site's current claims, not an independent product test. The functional bundle is substantially more work than a checklist.

Mohit's [Medium article](https://medium.com/@hii_mohit/i-found-the-best-macbook-notch-apps-in-2026-ca713ebc6120) compares seven notch apps, places NotchBuddy first and links to its website. The author profile displayed 8.2K followers. It reaches readers already considering that product category. I did not see a clear ownership disclosure in the article body reviewed. CoachRank should explicitly disclose ownership in its own promotional comparisons. A subsequent browser review of his [X profile](https://x.com/hii_mohit) showed 7,579 followers and several recent product and marketing updates. In a [September 19 post](https://x.com/hii_mohit/status/2101280699680694389), he said 50% of his sales came from his Medium articles. That is a founder-reported attribution claim, with no time window or independent channel verification shown. It should not be restated as proof that the single linked article generated half of NotchBuddy's revenue.

## Applications for CoachRank

1. **Show the useful result.** Demonstrate a single Brand Clarity priority and its action, followed by the seven-day plan. Keep fictional examples labeled. A screenshot should help someone evaluate the output without reading a long pitch.
2. **Connect the article to an immediate decision.** The new brand-audit and positioning guides are close to a buyer's current task. Start distribution there, and connect the paid assessment to the broader self-review it actually provides.
3. **Make the exchange concrete.** State the input, completion time, exact output, price and access terms. Avoid implying a questionnaire automatically evaluates the visitor's actual website.
4. **Use several distribution surfaces thoughtfully.** Publish useful standalone exercises on X and Reddit. Adapt or import one owned article to Medium, with a canonical link to CoachRank and an ownership disclosure. Measure the resulting visits and purchases.
5. **Keep the audience difference in view.** A founder with an existing readership has distribution assets that a new publication must build. Posting the same kind of article does not reproduce those conditions.
6. **Let evidence guide the next small product.** A $3 checklist needs to save effort through a useful output. Price alone does not turn generic advice into an attractive purchase. The current recommendation remains a short promotion-readiness check that assembles specific actions, with a complete sample available first.

The credible lesson is that a concrete, demonstrable outcome can be paired with content that reaches people considering that outcome. The founder reports meaningful Medium sales, but the available evidence does not establish how much revenue the single linked article generated. CoachRank's new buying-journey measurements are intended to make our own channel results observable.
