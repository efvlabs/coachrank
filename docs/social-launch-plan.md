# CoachRank: first readers and first sale

Prepared 6 September 2026. This is a proposed launch plan, not a publishing log. The private Social desk is at https://coachrank.lol/admin/social. Drafts and account suggestions are saved separately from completed posts and follows.

## Start with a focused audience

CoachRank can cover five editorial topics while promoting a specific first product to a narrower audience: independent coaches, consultants, creators and founders whose offer is difficult to explain. The $9 assessment is the entry point. Lead with the useful work behind the product: clear questions, a sample report and concrete next actions. Never promise that buying it will produce sales.

Focus only on X for this launch. Use original observations, useful article extracts and conversations with relevant readers. Reddit, Medium, LinkedIn and other channels are a later phase, after we know which topics attract interested readers and which product questions prevent purchases. These priorities are strategic recommendations, not platform reach guarantees.

## Seven posts, one considered sequence

The Social desk contains seven editable X drafts. Dates are intentionally blank so you can choose your launch day. Every draft has a source, visual direction and an intended role. Copy post adds a source link with X-specific UTM tags. Check the final copy, image, link and length in the platform composer before publishing.

1. Pin the point of view: CoachRank is an independent editorial for ambitious people. Use the wordmark or a strong cover.
2. Give readers a useful brand exercise. Link to Clarity before identity.
3. Show the assessment's fictional sample report. Clearly label the visual as a sample.
4. Share a decision journal exercise that stands on its own.
5. Open a thoughtful conversation about listening in coaching.
6. Give creative work a practical finish line.
7. Explain the $9 offer plainly, with a product link and its real inclusions.

One good original post each day is the starting experiment. Spend another 15 to 20 minutes reading and replying to relevant posts. Add a concrete example or thoughtful question. Adjust the pace to what you can maintain. Do not bulk-follow, mass-DM, copy comments across accounts or put sales links into unrelated conversations. Avoid generic motivational quotes, engagement bait and invented success stories.

## Search Console checklist

1. Open the verified coachrank.lol property in Google Search Console. A domain property covers www and non-www, HTTP and HTTPS; verify via DNS if no property exists yet.
2. Under Sitemaps, submit `https://coachrank.lol/sitemap.xml` once. It already includes the five editorial topic pages, public tools, rankings and all indexable published articles. New published articles join it automatically. The sitemap is also declared in robots.txt.
3. Use URL Inspection for the four priority pages below. Test the live URL and request indexing if it is not indexed or the indexed version is outdated.
4. Monitor Page indexing for exclusions and Performance for actual impressions/clicks. Do not repeatedly resubmit the same URL. Check a representative article from each topic over the following weeks.

Priority URLs:

- https://coachrank.lol/
- https://coachrank.lol/tools/brand-clarity
- https://coachrank.lol/blog/brand-clarity-before-brand-identity
- https://coachrank.lol/blog/decision-journal-before-the-outcome

You do not need to manually request all 35 articles. Use the sitemap for bulk discovery. Do not request private admin, API, assessment access, checkout, or personal report URLs. Submit clean canonical URLs without UTM parameters. Indexing can take days or weeks and is not guaranteed. We can verify public crawl signals, but the site's actual Google indexing status requires access to the verified Search Console property.

Sources: [Google: request crawling and submit sitemaps](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl), [Google: canonical URLs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls), [Search Console property types](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/34592).

## What the Social desk tracks

Publishing queue: channel, working title, post copy, source article/tool, draft/ready/published/archive status, planned date, published date, live link and notes. Copy post adds a tracked destination link. A published record requires a live URL and a completed date.

People & ideas: account URL, channel, reason to follow, research source, research/following/archive status, actual followed date and notes.

Article distribution: the published editorial library and the social records linked to each article. Create a connected draft directly from a story.

Launch playbook: X cadence, research approach and indexing checklist in the console.

This is a persistent manual planning and logging workspace. Planned dates do not schedule or send posts. It does not follow accounts, read private social data or pull platform analytics. Archive preserves old records and they can be restored by changing status. Simultaneous edits are protected against silently overwriting each other.

## Review after seven days

Log relevant replies, repeated questions, available link-click numbers and confirmed purchases. UTM tags are included in outbound URLs, but they do not provide analytics on their own. Use your available analytics and actual payment records; do not infer sales from views.

If readers click through but do not understand what they receive, improve the product demonstration. If few relevant people visit, improve the topic and conversations before changing the price. When someone buys, seek honest feedback through a channel they have agreed to use. Publish a testimonial only with their permission and their real words.
