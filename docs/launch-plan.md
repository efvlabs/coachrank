# CoachRank launch

CoachRank is an independent editorial for ambitious people: celebrating greatness and understanding what builds it. The five topics are Performance, Business, Creativity, Growth and Coaching. Branding and productivity are tags. Start with useful ideas and original exercises; profiles can follow when reporting and access justify them. Editorial selection is independent of paid ranking placement.

The `.lol` domain adds personality without requiring a joke in every headline. The editorial line is “Serious about growth. Light on the ego.” The refined Rise mark is three solid shapes around a summit, with a consistent cobalt tile and a clean wordmark. It is a proposed identity direction, not a claim of trademark clearance.

## First product

Brand Clarity Assessment: $9 USD once, plus applicable tax. 24 behavior-anchored questions across Audience, Offer, Difference, Proof, Message and Visibility. Buyers receive a visual report, three answer-based priorities, a seven-day plan, a downloadable PDF and lifetime access, unlimited personal retakes and side-by-side comparisons of saved reports. No subscription. The public sample demonstrates exactly what buyers receive.

The assessment is an original structured reflection exercise. Scores are not scientific norms or promises of commercial performance. Do not describe the report as AI generated: it uses transparent, deterministic scoring and authored recommendations.

## First-sale sequence

1. Review the five live articles and run the full no-charge assessment from Admin Studio → Tools & feedback. Download the PDF and verify that each recommendation is useful.
2. Publish the brand-clarity article from the founder's X account with a concrete problem-led message. Link to the article first; its product call to action leads to the sample and checkout.
3. Publish a second post showing a clearly labeled sample dimension chart and the $9 offer. Give enough information to evaluate the purchase: who it serves, what it contains, and what it does not claim.
4. Follow up on relevant public conversations with a helpful observation. Share the link when it answers the question; avoid repetitive promotional replies and unsolicited direct messages.
5. Read voluntary buyer feedback in Admin Studio. Check that payments produce access, buyers finish reports, and they can name a useful action. Improve the product from those observations before adding more products.

No sale, conversion rate or audience growth is guaranteed. The initial launch is a way to learn whether this particular promise earns a purchase.

## What comes next

Keep the product range focused until paid buyers demonstrate demand for a specific follow-on outcome. A $29 workbook or $49 positioning kit can deepen the work; a $99 guided implementation package needs substantially more value than a longer questionnaire. Decide from buyer feedback, completion and repeat use, rather than a calendar deadline.

Defer a marketplace until the editorial reaches a recognizable audience and vendors ask to reach it. When built, separate paid vendor placements from editorial recommendations and keep its bid totals separate from coach rankings and tool sales.
