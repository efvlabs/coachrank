# Small products to test before a larger tool

Status: recommendations only. No new product, checkout or price has been created.

The founder confirmed on September 20, 2026 that CoachRank has no paid sales yet. Prioritize one narrow, usable result over expanding the product range. A low price is an experiment in willingness to pay; it does not establish demand or remove sign-in and checkout friction.

## Recommended first experiment: Before You Promote Check

Proposed price: $3 USD once, before applicable tax. Audience: a solo founder about to share a paid offer on Reddit, X or another channel.

Promise: complete a short launch check and leave with the gaps to resolve before sending people to your offer.

The first version should have 12 checks, using Done, Not yet and Not checked. Each question should explain how the user can verify the item. Group the checks into offer information, buying path and delivery. Examples include whether the page names the deliverable, whether the call to action reaches the promised destination, whether payment frequency is visible, and whether a verified purchase receives access. Mark a payment or delivery step as untested until the owner has appropriate evidence.

The result should show the user's reported blockers, the unknowns that still need checking, a small ordered action list and a downloadable record. Explain why a step comes first. Do not invent a conversion score or describe the user's answers as an automated website audit. If every check passes, show a completion record and suggest an observation to collect from the next promotion. Do not manufacture a problem to justify the purchase.

One useful result, one page, one saved check and retakes. Reuse the existing account and payment foundation while making ownership product-specific. No AI, website crawling, competitor research, external integrations or ongoing monitoring is needed for the first version.

What earns the price: a guided sequence that remembers the answers and assembles a useful personal action sheet. A generic PDF of advice already available in the free articles would be too weak. Show a complete sample with real guidance before asking for payment.

This is narrower than Brand Clarity: it focuses on the immediate readiness of one promotion and buying journey. Brand Clarity remains the broader $9 reflection across audience, offer, difference, proof, message and visibility.

A $1 downloadable checklist is the weakest option: limited differentiation, the same checkout steps and less room for transaction fees. Pricing should follow the useful result rather than become the main selling point.

## First-sale test

Prepare one complete sample and a short mobile-friendly explanation. State the full price and what happens after purchase before sign-in. Retain the free article exercises in full. Do not reduce them to push a purchase.

Before attributing zero sales to price, inspect the existing route from product page to sign-in, checkout, payment confirmation and access. Project notes previously identified shared Firebase email branding and a sign-in email landing in spam. That is a recorded concern, not a fresh verification; review it separately before changing the shared authentication configuration.

When a new product is authorized, measure product visits, checkout starts, successful payments and completed outputs separately. Ask early buyers which action they used. Voluntary feedback and a completed useful result matter more than a hypothetical survey response or social likes. Make a few sales before deciding whether to build a larger product.
