# Edition 02 design decisions

The complete CoachRank wordmark appears in the header with the editorial tagline. Removing the giant duplicate masthead brings the lead article closer to the navigation. The live activity pill retains its central position and truthful database totals. The small C. mark is reserved for the favicon, where the complete name is not legible.

The product page demonstrates its outcome with an illustrative interactive report, six dimension icons, a sample question and a clear one-time purchase. The full sample remains accessible before payment. The comparison sample is labeled fictional and is not presented as a customer result.

The assessment groups 24 questions into six sections. Progress, saved state and return access are visible. Optional explanations sit beside each question. These choices follow the principles of [W3C's multi-page forms guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/) and [Nielsen Norman Group's progressive disclosure guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/). Reports preserve the same scoring version for useful comparisons. The product is an authored business reflection tool, not a scientifically validated instrument.

The visual reference review included [Linear](https://linear.app/) and [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/payments), especially their use of product demonstrations and information hierarchy. CoachRank retains its own typography, colors, original geometric artwork and editorial voice.

Validation covers lint, types, production compilation, automated payment and entitlement tests, HTTP routes and metadata, private report access, history pagination, downloadable PDFs and visual inspection of exported artwork and PDFs. It does not establish a conversion rate or guarantee commercial results.
