## CoachRank writing rules

Never use em dashes (Unicode U+2014) in CoachRank-authored content. This applies to website copy, articles, headlines, excerpts, metadata, alt text, admin labels, assessments, reports, documentation and promotional drafts. Do not use HTML entities or Unicode escapes to introduce an em dash into rendered copy.

Rewrite the sentence with periods, commas, colons or parentheses, or remove the break when a simple conjunction works. Do not mechanically substitute another long dash. Preserve normal hyphens in compound words and URLs. Review both repository content and stored article fields when making site-wide copy changes.

This rule applies to authored content. Preserve the Next.js-managed instructions above and character matching in text-normalization code.
